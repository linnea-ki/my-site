About

I am a Research Assistant at Karolinska Institutet working on host–microbiome interactions. My work combines laboratory characterisation of gut and vaginal isolates (MALDI-TOF, antibiotic susceptibility testing) with sequencing-based analysis (16S rRNA amplicon and whole-genome sequencing).

Professional summary

Biomedicine graduate with hands-on research experience in microbiology, oncology, and reproductive health, from bacterial and phage characterization to gene regulation in cancer cell lines and microRNA function in 3D endometrial models. My greatest motivation is closing research gaps in female-specific health conditions such as PMDD and hormonal disorders. Additional background in quality assurance and structured process development from Klarna.
