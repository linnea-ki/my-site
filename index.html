About

I am a Research Assistant at Karolinska Institutet working on host–microbiome interactions. My work combines laboratory characterisation of gut and vaginal isolates (MALDI-TOF, antibiotic susceptibility testing) with sequencing-based analysis (16S rRNA amplicon and whole-genome sequencing).
